Our Philosophy

Where Little Moments
Become Heirlooms

Shia Baby began with a simple belief: the smallest wardrobe in the house deserves the most thoughtful pieces. Every swaddle, every knitted blanket, every hand-finished keepsake in our boutique is chosen the way you would choose it — slowly, carefully, and with the people you love in mind.

Inspired by storybook illustrations, vintage toile, and the quiet elegance of European nurseries, our collections are made to be worn, photographed, kept — and one day, passed down.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

When does Shia Baby open? +

We're currently preparing our boutique in the Mercado District, with online shopping following soon after. Join the Founding Families list and we'll email you the moment doors open — online and in person.

What do I get for joining the Founding Families list? +

Early access before the public launch, a first look at new arrivals and gifting guides, and 15% off your first order as a founding member.

What is the Gender Reveal Experience? +

The Reveal Atelier is our luxury gender reveal service — a keepsake box with a personalized reveal song and a beautifully wrapped outfit, designed to turn the moment into a cinematic memory. It's available to book now, ahead of the boutique's launch.

Do you offer gift wrapping? +

Every Shia Baby order will arrive gift-ready in our signature packaging at no charge, with an optional handwritten gift note at checkout.

Are your fabrics safe for newborns? +

Yes. We're curating pieces in premium natural fibers — organic cottons, soft muslins, fine knits — chosen for delicate new skin.
